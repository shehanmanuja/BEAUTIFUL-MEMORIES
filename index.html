Forever & Always
My little Chabee,

Thank you for every beautiful memory, every smile, every moment, and every little thing that became special because of you.

I know there were times when I showed you my anger. But behind that anger was someone who was deeply afraid of losing the person he loved so much. I was scared of losing you, and sometimes I didn't know how to express that fear properly. I never wanted to hurt you. I never wanted to make you feel like you were a bad person, and I never wanted you to think that I could see you that way. I'm sorry for the moments when my feelings came out in the wrong way.

Maybe you understood me differently from how I wanted you to. I know I cannot change what you felt, and I respect your feelings, too.

Chabee, whenever you think of me, I hope you'll remember this little page. Open this link whenever you miss our memories, whenever you want to remember us, or whenever you simply want to see a little part of our story again. Everything here exists because those moments meant so much to me.

I'm always with you in my heart, even if I'm far away.

Sometimes, I feel like my presence in your life might be holding you back, and that is the last thing I ever want to do. I never want my love to become a burden to you. I want you to be happy, to grow, to follow your dreams, and to live your life freely.

But if your heart ever wants to find its way back to me, please know that you don't have to be afraid to come to me. You don't have to have everything figured out. You don't have to pretend to be okay. Your Shehan is still here, with the same love and care he always wanted to give you.

I never needed anything extraordinary from you. Your love, your care, your little smiles, your time, and the feeling that I mattered to you were enough to make me happy. Those little things meant the world to me.

And Chabee, if there is one thing I wish you would never take away from our story, it is the love we shared. I don't want to lose the little connection that reminds me that we were once such an important part of each other's lives.

Please don't forget me completely. Please don't let our memories become something that never mattered. I know life can change, and I know I cannot ask you to feel something you no longer feel. But I hope there will always be a little corner in your heart where our story remains.

You know my story, too. You know the parts of me that I don't show to everyone. You knew my heart, my fears, and the person I was behind everything. When you loved me, I believed that I had finally found someone who understood me and wouldn't leave me feeling alone with everything I carried inside.

That's why this means so much to me, Chabee. The thought of losing you and having to face that emptiness alone scares me more than I can explain.

I'm not asking you to feel guilty, and I'm not asking you to come back because you feel sorry for me. If you ever choose me again, I want it to be because your heart genuinely wants me there.

But if there is still love in your heart for me, even a little, I hope you won't be afraid to tell me.

One day, if you can look into my heart again and say just three words — “I love you, Shehan” — I think that moment would mean more to me than a thousand other things.

Those words are not something I can demand from you. I just want you to know how deeply I would treasure them if they ever came from your heart again.

No matter how many days pass, the memories we created will always have a special place in mine. Every photograph, every smile, every conversation, and every little moment will always remind me of the happiness we once shared.

I don't know what the future holds for us. I don't know whether our paths will meet again or whether life has a different plan. But I know that the love I felt for you was real, and the memories I made with you will always mean something to me.

I'm still here, Chabee. I'm waiting with hope, not because I want to stop you from living your life, but because a part of my heart still wishes that our story might have another chapter.

If you ever want to talk, if you ever miss me, or if you ever feel that you want to try again, you can reach out to me. You don't have to be perfect, and we don't have to have all the answers. We can simply start by talking honestly, understanding each other, and taking things one step at a time.

Thank you for being part of my story. Thank you for every memory, every smile, every moment, and every little thing that became special because of you.

My love will never end.

And somewhere in my heart, a little part of me will always be hoping for us — for as long as that hope remains.

Forever yours, ❤

Your Shehan.
